Privacy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

The short version

The tool runs entirely in your browser. The HTML and URLs you audit never leave your computer. We collect nothing.

What is stored on your device

One preference: if you change the appearance, it is saved in your browser's local storage under the name ada-theme. It never leaves your device. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Third parties

  • Hosting: this site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub's own privacy terms (opens in new tab) apply to the hosting.
  • Donations: if you use the PayPal donate link, PayPal handles the payment and its privacy policy (opens in new tab) applies. We never see your card details.
  • Advertising and analytics: this site currently has no ads and no analytics. If that ever changes, this page will say so first.

Contact
